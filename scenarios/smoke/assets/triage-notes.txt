Checkout double-charge triage notes
===================================

Reproduced on the v1 checkout path with two browser tabs submitting the same
cart within roughly 200ms of each other. Both submissions were accepted and the
customer was charged twice.

What we know:

- The cart lock is taken after the payment authorisation, not before it.
- The second submission sees a cart that still reads as unpaid.
- Refunds have to be issued by hand today; there is no automatic reversal.

Next steps: confirm whether the billing side observes one invoice or two, and
whether the dunning retry path can compound the problem.
